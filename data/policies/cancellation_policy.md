# Order Cancellation Policy

## Before shipping
You can cancel an order for free from the My Orders page any time before it is shipped. The refund is processed in 3-5 business days.

## After shipping
Once an order is shipped it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse delivery when the courier arrives, or return the item after delivery as per the return policy of that category.

## Cancellation by us
We may cancel an order if the product is out of stock or the payment fails. A full refund is issued automatically.
