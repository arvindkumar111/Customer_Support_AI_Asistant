# Failed Payments and Payment Deducted but Order Failed

## What happens
Sometimes money is deducted from your account but the order is not confirmed. This happens because of a bank or network timeout.

## Automatic reversal
The amount is automatically reversed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. You do not need to place the order again or make a second payment.

## If the amount is not reversed
If the money has not come back after 7 business days, contact support with your transaction ID and a bank statement screenshot. The support team will raise a dispute with the payment gateway.

## Safety note
Our team will never ask for your card number, CVV, OTP or UPI PIN.
