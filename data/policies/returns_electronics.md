# Return Policy: Electronics

## Standard returns
Electronics such as phones, laptops and tablets can be returned within 7 days of delivery if the product is unused and in original packaging.

## Damaged or defective items
If an electronic item arrives damaged, the customer must report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the product and packaging. A free pickup will be arranged and a replacement or full refund will be issued.

## Non-returnable items
Opened software, gift cards and personal care electronics are not eligible for return.
