# Warranty Policy: Phones, Laptops and Accessories

## Warranty period
- Smartphones: 1 year manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery.
- Laptops: 1 year manufacturer warranty. Extended warranty of 1 or 2 years can be bought within 30 days of purchase.
- Accessories such as chargers, cables and earphones: 6 months.

## What is covered
Manufacturing defects and hardware faults that occur under normal use.

## What is not covered
Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repairs, software issues caused by third-party apps, and normal wear and tear are not covered.

## How to claim
Visit the nearest brand authorised service centre with the invoice and the product. We do not repair devices ourselves. Our support team can share the service centre list for your city.
