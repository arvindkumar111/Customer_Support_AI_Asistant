# Refund Policy

## Refund method
Refunds are always sent to the original payment method. We cannot send a refund to a different bank account, UPI ID or card shared over chat.

## Refund timelines
- UPI and net banking: 5-7 business days after the returned item passes quality check or the order is cancelled.
- Credit and debit cards: 7-10 business days.
- Wallet balance: instant after approval.
- Cash on delivery orders: refunded to the in-app wallet, or to a bank account verified with OTP in the app, within 7-10 business days.

## Quality check
After pickup, the item goes through a quality check that takes 1-2 business days. The refund starts only after the item passes.

## Refund approval
Refunds above Rs 5000 need manual approval by a support specialist before they are processed.
