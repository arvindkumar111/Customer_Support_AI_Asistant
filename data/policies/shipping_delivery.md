# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Delivery time
- Standard delivery: 3-7 business days.
- Express delivery: 1-2 business days in selected cities.
Delivery on Sundays is not guaranteed and depends on the courier partner and your pincode.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free for orders of Rs 499 and above. Orders below Rs 499 have a shipping charge of Rs 40.

## Delayed orders
If your order is not delivered within 10 days of the order date, you can cancel it and get a full refund.

## Failed delivery attempts
The courier makes 3 delivery attempts. After the third failed attempt the order is returned to us and refunded.
